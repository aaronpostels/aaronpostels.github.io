Project Overview

FrogYou! is a fast-paced 2D precision platformer where players control a nimble ninja frog navigating increasingly hazardous mechanical environments. The objective in each level is simple yet demanding: collect every fruit as quickly as possible to earn up to 3 stars per stage.

Along with responsive ground and aerial movement, the frog possesses two core abilities: the Spore Blast (an explosive directional dash) and the Vine Grappler (a tether for swinging across yawning chasms and slingshotting through enemies). A key mechanic for speedrunning is that collecting any fruit instantly resets all ability cooldowns, rewarding players who chain dashes and grappling maneuvers into continuous, high-speed routes.

The game features 8 hand-crafted levels that transition from vibrant organic landscapes into hostile robotic factories, complete with chasing drone Spyders, rotating saws, ghost run recording, and online global leaderboards.

Key Features

  • Precision Movement & Abilities: Responsive jump physics complemented by an 8-directional Spore Blast dash and dynamic Vine Grappler tether.
  • Fruit Cooldown Reset: Collecting fruit instantly replenishes ability charges, unlocking high-skill route optimization and momentum chaining.
  • Ghost Recording System: Custom telemetry system records player run trajectories and renders ghost replays against personal bests.
  • Online Leaderboards: Integrated LootLocker backend syncing high scores, best completion times, and star progression.
  • Adaptive Audio & Visuals: Velocity-scaled camera framing, screen shake on impacts, pitch-incrementing collection chimes, and retro pixel art aesthetic.
  • 8 Progressive Levels: Stages scale from open routing tutorials to high-density gauntlets featuring moving platforms, pendulums, and flying Spyders.
