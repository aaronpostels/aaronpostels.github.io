## Bachelor Thesis

**Entwicklung eines ereignisgesteuerten Animationssystems für performative Bewegungsabläufe am Beispiel autonomer Torjubel im Roboterfußball**
(Development of an event-driven animation system for performative motion sequences, using autonomous goal celebrations in robot football as example)

B.Sc. Digital Media, University of Bremen, submitted 23.07.2026. Written in German. Supervisors: Prof. Dr.-Ing. Udo Frese, Nuri Ovüc.

Summary: A workflow from a motion reference to execution on the physical Booster K1 robot. A source video is reconstructed as 3D human motion and retargeted to the K1. A custom Blender add-on, "K1 Animator", makes the motion editable on a constrained robot rig. Dynamic full-body dances are trained in Isaac Lab (reinforcement learning). Simple head and arm gestures run as keyframe animations. Both are integrated into the B-Human behavior and motion architecture, can be triggered by game events, test commands or a controller, and were tested on the physical K1.
