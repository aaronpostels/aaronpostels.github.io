4. Cookies & Third-Party Services

The core pages of this portfolio do not set cookies or use local tracking storage. Some demos store settings or game state in your browser's localStorage (e.g. the terminal name in Eneida); this data never leaves your device (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

YouTube Videos: Videos on case study pages use a 2-click mechanism with privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). Connection to YouTube/Google servers is established only after you click, based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG).

Firebase Firestore (Eneida Demo): Opening the Eneida demo does not contact Google. Only when you view the leaderboard or save a highscore are the Firebase libraries loaded from Google servers (www.gstatic.com) and data read from Firestore, which transmits your IP address to Google; saving a highscore sends your chosen nickname, score, and a timestamp to Google. Nicknames are publicly visible on the leaderboard (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; US transfers based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework).

CDN libraries in demos: Some demos under /projects/ (e.g. uSeek, Particles, Scroll Scrub, Hytale) load libraries from jsDelivr, cdnjs (Cloudflare) and unpkg. Your IP address is transmitted to these providers (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The uSeek demo's 3D scenes from Spline (USA) load only after you click "3D-Szene laden"; only then are the Spline runtime, the scene and Google Fonts files requested (consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). The main portfolio pages and case studies do not contact these providers.
